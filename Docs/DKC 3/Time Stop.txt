DKC 3 Time Stop:

$194B	time stop flags
$194D	time stop timer

bit 0 (0x0001)	stop camera
bit 1 (0x0002)	unknown
bit 2 (0x0004)	stop non kong sprites
bit 3 (0x0008)	stop active kong
bit 4 (0x0010)	stop inactive kong
bit 5 (0x0020)	stop all sprites
bit 6 (0x0040)	unknown
bit 7 (0x0080)	stop all sprites
bit 8 (0x0100)
bit 9 (0x0200)
bit 10 (0x0400)
bit 11 (0x0800)
bit 12 (0x1000)
bit 13 (0x2000)
bit 14 (0x4000)
bit 15 (0x8000)
